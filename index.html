Arthur Pendelton

"My heart, my love, and my lifetime of memories stay with you forever."

Life Story

Early Life & Youth

Arthur was born in a quiet country town where he spent his youth exploring nature and learning the values of hard work, humility, and kindness from his family.

Family & Marriage

In 1968, he married the love of his life, Eleanor. Together they built a warm, welcoming home filled with laughter, dedication, and two wonderful children.

Passions & Legacy

An avid gardener and passionate woodworker, Arthur dedicated his retired years to crafting wooden toys for his grandchildren and cultivating beautiful roses.
